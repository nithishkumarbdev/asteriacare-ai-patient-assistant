# Departments and Doctors

## Cardiology

Located at Riverside Campus. Dr. Aisha Kapoor and Dr. Marcus Webb see
patients for hypertension, chest pain evaluation, arrhythmia, and routine
cardiac follow-up. New patients should expect to bring any prior ECG or
cardiac test results if available, though it isn't required to book.

## Orthopedics

Located at Riverside Campus. Dr. Elena Torres treats joint pain, sports
injuries, fractures, and post-surgical follow-up. Same-week appointments are
usually available for acute injuries.

## Pediatrics

Located at Northgate Clinic. Dr. Sam Okafor sees patients from birth through
age 17 for wellness checks, vaccinations, and general pediatric concerns.

## Dermatology

Located at Northgate Clinic. Dr. Priya Nair handles skin conditions, acne,
mole checks, and minor dermatological procedures.

## General Medicine

Available at both Riverside Campus and Northgate Clinic. General Medicine
is the right starting point for patients who aren't sure which specialist
they need — the physician can refer within AsteriaCare as needed.
