# Insurance and Policies

## Accepted Insurance

AsteriaCare accepts most major insurance plans, including Blue Shield,
Aetna, and UnitedHealthcare, at all three locations. Patients without
insurance can request a self-pay estimate before their appointment.

## Rescheduling and Cancellations

Appointments can be rescheduled or canceled up to 24 hours in advance
without a fee. Cancellations within 24 hours of the appointment may incur a
missed-appointment fee, waived on request for emergencies.

## New Patient Paperwork

New patients are asked to arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake
paperwork, or can complete it online in advance via the patient portal link
sent after booking.
