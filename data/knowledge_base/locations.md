# AsteriaCare Hospital Locations

## Riverside Campus

AsteriaCare Riverside Campus is our flagship location, offering Cardiology,
Orthopedics, General Medicine, and 24/7 Emergency care. Outpatient hours are
Monday–Saturday, 8am–7pm. Walk-in registration is available for Emergency;
all other departments require an appointment.

## Northgate Clinic

AsteriaCare Northgate is a smaller outpatient clinic focused on Pediatrics,
Dermatology, and General Medicine. It does not have an Emergency department;
patients needing emergency care should go to Riverside Campus. Hours are
Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm.

## Eastside Diagnostic Center

AsteriaCare Eastside specializes in imaging and lab diagnostics — X-ray, MRI,
CT, and full blood panels. Most tests require a referral from an AsteriaCare
doctor. Walk-in blood draws are available without a referral, Monday–Saturday,
7am–4pm.
